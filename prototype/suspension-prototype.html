Design targets

Tick what you want, type a value (or a range such as 9..12) and press Solve. Solve moves the unlocked hardpoints as little as possible. With a range it only moves until the value is inside it. The proposal is shown in amber, in the views and in the charts.

    How to use

    • Click a point in a view, or a row in the table. Drag the arrows to move it in 1 mm steps, or type exact values.
    • Design targets: tick a quantity, set the value you want and press Solve. Lock the points that must stay where they are (chassis rails, wheel centre). Some targets are linked: scrub, spindle length and KPI depend on each other through the tyre radius, so set at most two of them.
    • Isometric view: drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Front and side views: drag to pan, scroll to zoom.
    • Blue curve = current design. Dashed grey = baseline. Set a baseline, change something, and compare. The baseline is kept when you switch between double wishbone and MacPherson.
    • Front view: instant centre (IC) and roll centre (RC). Side view: the side-view instant centre (SVIC) behind the anti-dive or anti-squat figure.
    • Red dash-dot line = steering axis. On a MacPherson strut it runs from the top mount to the lower ball joint.
    • ISO 8855 axes. Negative camber = top of the wheel leans in. Toe-in is positive.

    Engine checks

    Each check runs the solver on a simple geometry and compares the result with a value worked out by hand.
